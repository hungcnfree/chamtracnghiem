SAN PHAM CHAM TRAC NGHIEM BANG CAMERA - HUONG DAN DAT AUDIO VA DAY LEN GITHUB

PHAN 1. TAO AUDIO (thay cho giong may, tuy chon)
1. Mo CIT Voice Studio, vao tab "Tao hang loat".
2. Thu truoc: dan 3 dong dau cua loi-thoai/thoai-1.txt, chon giong (khuyen nghi Adam bua), xuat WAV, nghe thu.
3. Dan nguyen file loi-thoai/thoai-1.txt (60 moc P1-P60), xuat WAV, tai file .zip va giai nen.
4. Lam tuong tu voi loi-thoai/thoai-2.txt (moc P61-P118).
   Luu y: KHONG bam nut "Lam sach" khi dan file. Moi moc [P..] phai nam tren mot dong rieng.
5. Chep TAT CA file .wav (ten dang 001-P1.wav, 002-P2.wav...) vao thu muc "audio" trong thu muc nay.
   Khong can doi ten: file dang-len-github se tu doi ten theo audio\map.csv.

PHAN 2. DAY LEN GITHUB
1. Tren github.com tao mot kho (repository) moi, de Public, KHONG tick them README. Chep dia chi .git cua kho.
2. Bam dup file dang-len-github.bat. Lan dau no hoi ten, email va dia chi kho; cac lan sau chay thang.
   Neu may hien cua so dang nhap GitHub, dang nhap la xong.
3. Tren GitHub: Settings > Pages > Source "Deploy from a branch", Branch "main", thu muc "/ (root)" > Save.
4. Doi 1-2 phut, trang chay tai https://TEN-BAN.github.io/TEN-KHO/ (co HTTPS nen camera dien thoai moi hoat dong).

PHAN 3. SU DUNG
- Tab "Cham": bam "Giong noi: tat" de bat. Thieu file audio nao thi app tu dung giong may cho cau do.
- Moi lan sua audio hoac sua app: chep file moi vao, bam dup dang-len-github.bat.

GHI CHU THANH THAT: bo cong cu nay chua duoc thu tren may cua thay (Windows, Git, GitHub Pages) va chua nghe thu giong that ghep tung doan.
